# CaseDrill scenario packs

`curated/` holds packs reviewed by the project maintainer.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `sample-uk-l2.xml` | Mixed queue of 8 alerts, UK, Level 2 |
| `sample-eu-l2.xml` | Mixed queue of 8 alerts, EU, Level 2 |
| `uk-l2-cash-business-review.xml` | One worked case. Its count of true positives reveals the outcome |
| `eu-l2-cash-business-review.xml` | The matching case with the opposite outcome |

All people, companies and events in these packs are fictional. The packs are provided for training only and are not legal advice.
